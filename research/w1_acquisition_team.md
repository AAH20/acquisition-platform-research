## 2. Culture

### Core Values (Official)
1. **Unimpeachable Character** — "Be the type of person with whom people are always proud to associate"
2. **Sincere Candor** — "Nothing great can be built without feedback"
3. **Competitive Greatness** — "Be at your best when your best is needed"

### Cultural Markers
- **"Only the very best need apply"** — high-bar recruiting posture
- **Remote-first / hybrid** — "We are remote. We work our asses off. We love what we do."
- **Certified Great Place to Work 2023**
- **Positive reinforcement culture** — Alex Hormozi explicitly contrasts "Mike" (praise-focused) vs. "Not Mike" (punitive) management styles
- **Psychological safety** — team presentations on Google's Project Aristotle findings
- **"Leave everything and everyone better than you found it"** — common internal phrase
- **Values-driven hiring** — "Our values aren't just words on a wall — we make every business decision through them, including who we hire"
- **Disciplined execution** — "Consistency beats intensity"
- **No politics, shortcuts, or excuses**

### Employee Sentiment
- "There's no other company I'd rather work for"
- "Our Leaders strive to ensure every employee has a voice"
- "I always feel connected to my team regardless of working virtually"
- "Alex and Leila set the standard of what it takes to win. We just try to keep up"

## 3. Hiring

### Process (4-Step)
1. **Video application** — 2–3 min intro video (no cover letter)
2. **Intro call** — 30 min with team
3. **Work sample** — scoped exercise close to actual role
4. **Offer & onboarding** — first few weeks structured onboarding

### What They Look For
- Disciplined team players who "trust and win together"
- High standards, ownership, attention to detail
- People over hype — trust, integrity, long-term thinking
- Competitive but collaborative — "share credit, support others"
- Growth through feedback and challenge
- Character and drive over credentials (Leila hired "Frank" from a stiff video because he cared deeply)

## 5. Remote Work

### Model
- **Hybrid** — "Work is about impact, not geography"
- Remote for focus and autonomy; in-person for strategy and relationships
- Mix of: Las Vegas onsite, Las Vegas hybrid, Remote USA
- Some roles fully remote (Sales, Talent Acquisition)
- Some roles fully onsite (Directors, Event Specialist)

### Remote Culture
- "I always feel connected to my team regardless of working virtually"
- Async-first with fewer meetings
- Results judged by output, not hours or location
- First full team meetup in Las Vegas (recorded for Spotify/YouTube)
- Structured huddles (sales-team-style energy huddles, not logistics check-ins)
- Regular event-based touchpoints with clients

### Challenges
- Communication gaps open up at scale
- Expectations go uncommunicated
- Onboarding remotely is difficult ("people quit on day five because they can't imagine onboarding every day")
- Maintaining energy and engagement virtually requires intentional design

## 6. Scaling

### Growth Trajectory
- Founded 2021
- 150+ employees by early 2026
- "Nearly doubled their team in a few months and is on track to double again"
- 35 portfolio companies, ~$4B enterprise value
- Portfolio revenue $200M+ annually

### Scaling Framework
- **WHO** — Recruit talent with winning track record
- **WHAT** — Lay out big initiatives, ruthlessly eliminate everything else
- **HOW** — Install step-by-step proven growth strategies into each function

### Scaling Systems
- Structured huddles (energy, not logistics)
- Event-based client touchpoints
- Cultural north star set by founders
- Structured team recognition (celebrating "competitive greatness")
- SOPs and deliverable tracking
- ACQ Vantage — verified virtual peer network for $1M+ operators
- AI-powered tools for scaling "the ACQ way"

### Portfolio Scaling Results (Self-Reported)
- Triple revenue in 5 years or less
- Increase monthly cash flow
- Develop core leadership team
- Grow from "Big Small Business" to "Valuable, Sellable Enterprise"

## 7. Bottlenecks

| Bottleneck | Detail |

| **Communication Breakdown** | Gaps open up when team scales quickly |
| **Onboarding at Scale** | New hires struggle; "people quit on day five" |
| **Hiring Speed vs. Quality** | Risk of wrong hires when scaling fast |
| **Team Departures** | Members send 9PM messages saying they might quit |
| **Event Staffing** | "Four team members call in sick the night before a major event" |
| **Founder Dependency** | Alex and Leila set the standard; hard to replicate as team grows |
| **Content Team Separation** | Leila separating content teams for Alex vs. her own brand |
| **Trust Erosion** | "Someone steals from the business, starts a competing company, or acts unethically" |
| **Mental Load** | "Legal battles, team departures, communication breakdowns, and the emotional weight of leading at scale" |

## 8. NP-Hard Problems

| Problem | Why It's NP-Hard |
|---|---|
| **Optimal Team Composition** | Finding the right mix of skills, culture fit, and cost for 150+ roles with interdependencies |
| **Scaling Culture Preservation** | Maintaining psychological safety, candor, and energy as team grows 10x — no known polynomial-time algorithm |
| **Hiring at Speed with Quality** | Balancing time-to-hire vs. quality-of-hire is a multi-objective optimization with no closed-form solution |
| **Remote/Hybrid Scheduling** | Coordinating onsite/hybrid/remote across time zones and role requirements |
| **Portfolio Company Selection** | Choosing which businesses to invest in (20–100% stakes) under uncertainty |
| **Content Team Separation** | Splitting overlapping brands (Alex vs. Leila) without losing synergies |
| **Onboarding Optimization** | Designing onboarding that works for remote, hybrid, and onsite simultaneously |
| **Leadership Transition** | Transferring founder vision/values to a new CEO without cultural drift |
