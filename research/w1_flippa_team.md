## 7. Bottlenecks

| Bottleneck | Description |
|---|---|
| **Marketplace Trust** | Malicious listings ("fishing for information") required speed bumps, review teams, and post-sales teams to combat |
| **Buyer-Seller Matching** | Original hands-off approach was flawed; sellers couldn't articulate business value to buyers |
| **Verification at Source** | Sellers lacked tools to share verified financials (QuickBooks integration added to address this) |
| **Valuation Complexity** | Digital asset valuation is inherently subjective; required building proprietary valuation tools |
| **Cross-Border Compliance** | Multi-entity, multi-jurisdiction operations (AU, UK, US, EU, Asia) create regulatory overhead |
| **Talent Density** | Competing for engineering talent against larger tech companies from a Melbourne/Austin base |
| **Low-Frequency Marketplace** | M&A is inherently low-frequency; paid marketing and user acquisition face structural challenges |

---

## 8. NP-Hard Problems

| Problem | Complexity | Flippa's Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Optimal Buyer-Seller Matching** | Combinatorial optimization over heterogeneous asset attributes, buyer preferences, price expectations, and timing constraints | AI-driven matching algorithms + human broker layer for high-value deals |
| **Digital Asset Valuation** | No standardized pricing model; depends on revenue multiples, growth rates, niche dynamics, and comparable sales | Proprietary valuation tool + broker expertise + QuickBooks verification |
| **Marketplace Trust at Scale** | Adversarial environment with information asymmetry between buyers and sellers | Verification at source, review teams, post-sales support, escrow, legal services integration |
| **Cross-Border M&A Execution** | Multi-jurisdiction legal, tax, and regulatory compliance per transaction | Integrated legal services, insurance, financing; regional broker partners |
| **Fraud Detection in Listings** | Adversarial actors creating malicious listings; requires continuous monitoring | Speed bumps, review teams, data verification pipelines |
