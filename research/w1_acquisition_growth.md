**Date:** 2026-10-04

**Scope:** Acquisition.com (Hormozi PE/advisory firm) + Acquire.com (online business marketplace)

## Executive Summary

The "Acquisition.com" name refers to two distinct entities that dominate the online business acquisition ecosystem:

1. **Acquisition.com** — Alex & Leila Hormozi's private investment and advisory firm (founded 2021, Las Vegas). Portfolio of 37 companies generating $250M+ annual revenue. New CEO Sharran Srivatsaa (ex-Goldman Sachs) is executing a multi-billion-dollar plan built on three engines: brand, capital, and operating involvement.

2. **Acquire.com** — Andrew Gazdecki's online marketplace for buying/selling internet businesses (founded 2020 as MicroAcquire, rebranded 2023). 500,000+ entrepreneurs, $500M+ closed deal volume, 2,000+ completed acquisitions. Expanded beyond SaaS to all profitable online businesses after acquiring MicroAssets.co.

Both entities are scaling aggressively through AI-first operating models, ecosystem strategies, and category expansion.

## 2. Expansion Plans

### Acquisition.com

- **ACQ Network** — Membership-based community launching 2026 at $5–10K/year. Includes AI-powered business consultant trained on real consulting data, industry-specific best practice sessions, and collective purchasing power (vendor rates on credit cards, agency services, business tools).
- **Ancillary Services** — Insurance, lending, and sales AI products for members.
- **Private Equity Expansion** — Taking 30–100% ownership stakes in companies that directly benefit from the ACQ distribution base.
- **Category King Thesis** — Buy, build, or partner to create the dominant company in each target market; 5–10 partnerships over next 10 years.
- **CAA Representation** — Signed with Creative Artists Agency to expand into high-level speaking engagements, digital/brand partnerships, podcasting, and live international touring.
- **Leadership Restructure** — Sharran Srivatsaa as CEO; Leila Hormozi as Executive Chairwoman; Evan Ageloff as President.
- **Vantage Program** — Virtual equivalent of in-person workshops for operators who can't travel to Las Vegas.

### Acquire.com

- **Category Expansion** — Expanded from SaaS-only to ecommerce, AI tools, newsletters, agencies, content, marketplaces, mobile apps, crypto, Shopify apps, and digital tools.
- **MicroAssets Acquisition** — Integrated 1,000+ small profitable online business listings and buyer base (June 2025).
- **Buyer Membership** — Now includes access to thousands of listings across all categories with AI-powered diligence tools, live concierge support, and Buyers University.
- **SBA 7(a) Financing** — Large share of $300K–$5M deals close with SBA loans; platform integrates financing guidance.
- **Transaction Volume** — Year-over-year volume up 40%+.

## 7. Bottlenecks & Strategic Challenges

### Acquisition.com

| Challenge | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Founder Dependency** | Alex Hormozi's personal brand is central; CAA deal mitigates but doesn't eliminate key-person risk |
| **Model Legitimacy** | Question of whether the model is "genuinely something new, or just private equity with a better social media strategy" |
| **PE Time Pressure** | Traditional PE's 3–6 year hold generates transaction costs eroding 5%+ of enterprise value per cycle; ACQ's longer hold may face similar pressures |
| **Brand Fragility** | The brand component is "the bet that makes this structurally different, and, some would argue, structurally fragile" |
| **Scale of Operating Involvement** | Active involvement in portfolio companies limits deal volume; trade-off between depth and breadth |
| **Leadership Transition** | New CEO (Srivatsaa) must prove the model works beyond Hormozi's personal involvement |
| **Real Estate Concentration** | $1B real estate target introduces market-cycle risk |

### Acquire.com

| Challenge | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Category Dilution Risk** | Expanding beyond SaaS may dilute the platform's core identity and buyer experience |
| **Quality Control** | Light vetting model (vs. Empire Flippers' heavy vetting) creates reputation risk as categories expand |
| **AI Disruption of Content Sites** | AI overviews compressing content site multiples — may reduce deal flow in that category |
| **Competitive Pressure** | Flippa has more weekly buyer traffic; Empire Flippers has stronger seller vetting; FE International is global |
| **Dependence on SBA Financing** | Changes in SBA 7(a) policy could significantly impact deal volume |
| **Non-US Expansion** | No clear roadmap for international growth; US-centric infrastructure |

## 8. NP-Hard Problems (Strategic Computational Challenges)

| Problem | Description |
|---|---|
| **Optimal Portfolio Construction** | Selecting 5–10 "category king" markets from hundreds of possibilities, each with different competitive dynamics, capital requirements, and synergy profiles — combinatorial optimization with interdependent variables |
| **Multi-Channel Service Delivery** | Determining optimal delivery modality mix (in-person, virtual, hybrid) across thousands of business owners with varying needs, price sensitivities, and geographic constraints — variant of the facility location problem |
| **AI Consultant Training** | Building an AI consultant trained on real consulting data that can generalize across industries, business models, and growth stages — knowledge representation and transfer learning at scale |
| **Real Estate Portfolio Optimization** | Allocating $1B across multifamily properties with varying risk/return profiles, geographic diversification, and co-investment structures — multi-objective portfolio optimization |
| **Membership Network Pricing** | Pricing ACQ Network at $5–10K/year while maximizing adoption, retention, and ancillary revenue — multi-sided platform pricing with network effects |
| **Deal Sourcing at Scale** | Matching 500,000+ entrepreneurs with verified buyers across dozens of categories, each with different diligence requirements — multi-dimensional matching problem |
| **Content-to-Revenue Funnel Optimization** | Optimizing the path from free content → books → School → advisory across millions of users with varying intent levels — multi-touch attribution and conversion optimization |
